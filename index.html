About

Practical, not theoretical

Angelo Consuls is focused on practical business solutions — digital tools, consulting resources and templates for entrepreneurs and organisations who need to get real work done.

Each product is built from actual consulting experience: registering businesses, writing plans that get approved, preparing tenders that get submitted, and solving the specific problems that come with running a business from Lesotho while working with clients internationally.

The goal is simple — save you time and give you a professional starting point, whether you're just getting started or refining an established business.
